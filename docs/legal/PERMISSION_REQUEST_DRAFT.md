**To:** copyright@take2games.com
**Subject:** Permission request: Leonida map in the independent RipTide companion app

Hello,

I develop RipTide Tools, an independently branded, free companion app for Grand Theft Auto players. It's available on our website and as desktop downloads for Windows, macOS and Linux. RipTide isn't affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive, and it doesn't access, read or modify the game.

RipTide includes a searchable map of Leonida with community-mapped places, progress checklists and personal markers. It can show raster tiles based on the official Leonida map, which we sourced through the community project gtadb.org. We understand that gtadb.org's Creative Commons licence covers its own work and not Rockstar's rights in the artwork, so we're asking you directly rather than relying on attribution or tolerance.

Would you grant written permission for RipTide to:

1. show the Leonida map within our app and website;
2. include a zoomable tiled version of it in our desktop downloads and in the website's cached files;
3. show players' own progress, personal markers, search and route planning on top of it (nothing that affects gameplay); and
4. use screenshots of those views to describe the app?

Please tell us any conditions: credit wording, where and how the map may appear, source files you'd prefer us to use, territories, duration, and how permission would end.

RipTide is free today. If we later add sponsorship, paid features or hosting, we'll ask separately and won't assume this permission covers them.

We can send the exact files and hashes, screenshots, the repository and download figures. If you can't permit this use, please let us know and we'll switch to our own map-free view, which keeps all player data.

Thank you for considering it.
